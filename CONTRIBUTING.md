# Contributing

Thanks for wanting to help!

- **Ideas and bugs**: [open an issue](../../issues). For bugs, say which Kindle and macOS you have, what you did and what you saw. The Kindle's log is `desk/desk.log` on the Kindle drive.
- **Pull requests**: welcome. Keep them small and focused, and check they build (see [docs/building.md](docs/building.md)). For anything big, open an issue first so we can talk about it.
- **Other Kindles**: the most wanted help. The layout is made for the Paperwhite 2 (1024 × 758, `/dev/input/event1`); making it adapt to other screens and touch panels is planned.

Desk Buddy is calm on purpose: black and white, minimal, no needless flashing, plain words. New features should feel the same.
